COURSE DISCRIPTION

This session in Urban Transportation Planning and Policy (CE73.13) covers a variety of themes, all related to transportation demand analysis and modeling. It deals both theories and examples for many kinds of transportation modes. They are important for transportation practitioners and urban planners. Many of the topics introduced in the session will be presented at the intermediate level. To have the knowledge and/or understandings about the microeconomic theory is highly recommended.
